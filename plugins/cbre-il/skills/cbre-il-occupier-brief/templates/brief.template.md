## What is driving the business right now
Four to six bullets, bold lead-in plus one or two sentences, each grounded in a dated commitment.

## The industry backdrop

## Where the next node probably goes
*CBRE analysis, not a <Company> disclosure. An inference throughout; it carries the error bar below.*
Two or three ranked clusters with their tier, growth-enabling before cost-reducing. Then:
- **The zone, not a town.** A corridor or a triangle between named places, with the back-test error attached.
- **The template**, from how they solved their last cluster: size band, tenure, standing stock or build, landlord type, self-operated or outsourced.
- **The window**, from the trigger and the lead time.
- **The error bar, measured.** Run as at a date before a node they actually took, and give the km error.
- **What would make this wrong.** The falsifier, stated before anyone checks.
- **Three things that would confirm it inside ninety days**, each with where to look.
