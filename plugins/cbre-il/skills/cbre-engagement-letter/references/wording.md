# Wording bank

Standard wording for each section, drawn from four issued letters: an occupier search
contracted in the Netherlands (2022), an occupier search in Romania (2023), a capital
markets sale and leaseback in Poland (2026) and a multi-country occupier search contracted
in the UK (2026). Adapt names, phases and specifics; keep the
structure and the tone. Replace [Client] with the short client name defined in the first
paragraph, e.g. "Example Client Ltd (Example)".

1. House style rules

## 1. House style rules

- British English (organisation, finalise, programme, centre).
- No em dashes or en dashes anywhere. Ranges use a hyphen: "3-6 months", "Minimum 8-10".
- Straight quotes and apostrophes.
- Full, readable sentences. Tables are for specifications; prose explains. No data dumps.
- Keep it relatively simple. High-level requirement, not a technical specification.
- "sq m" for areas, "m" for heights, "°C" for temperatures, "−" (minus sign) for negatives.
- Define the client short name once in the first paragraph and use it throughout.
- Numbers in contract terms are written twice: "12 (twelve) months", "30 (thirty) days".
- Never invent facts: unknown names, entities, addresses, fees and dates go in as
  `[[placeholder]]` (yellow in the letter).

## 2. Letter page

Opening (occupier search):
> Following our recent conversation, we are pleased to set out the basis on which CBRE will
> act for [Client full name] ([Client]) in its search for [one-line requirement]. [One or two
> sentences on why: the business need.] The search covers [geography].

Second paragraph: the one criterion that decides the search, and why the scope is shaped
the way it is. Then:
> This letter records the requirement, the services CBRE will provide, the team that will
> deliver them and how we are remunerated, together with the matters that fall outside our
> scope.

Signable letter:
> I should be grateful if you would confirm your acceptance of this appointment by signing
> and returning a copy of this letter. On receipt of the signed copy, we will start work on
> the instruction.

Scope-only letter (separate agreement follows):
> This letter is not itself the appointment. It sets out the agreed scope and commercial
> terms so that both sides are working from the same document. The binding appointment will
> be made under a separate advisory agreement, which [Entity] will issue for signature and
> into which the scope and the fee set out below will be carried over unchanged.

Always:
> We look forward to working with you. If in the meantime you have any questions on any
> aspect of the contents, please do not hesitate to contact us.

Closing greeting "Yours sincerely," then signatories (name bold, role italic, two per row).

## 3. The instruction

H1 "The instruction", H2 "The requirement" (occupier) or "The property" (capital markets).
One short paragraph, then a two-column "Item / Detail" table. Typical rows for an occupier
search: Purpose, Search area / Locations, Availability / Timing, Facility type, Size
(warehouse, office, mezzanine), Clear height, Temperature zones, Lease term, Other.

If there are alternative options with different specs, add H2 "Space requirements by
option" and a three-column table (Component / Option 1 / Option 2), preceded by:
> The figures below are indicative. We will not limit the search to them and will also
> present options that differ, where they still meet the operational need.

Italic note under a table only when it adds something the reader needs (source of areas,
a firm constraint). Do not add notes by default.

## 4. The services

### Occupier representation (default)

Intro:
> CBRE represents [Client] as occupier from the first market search through to signing the
> lease and handover of the building. The work runs in three phases.

H3 "Phase 1: Search and shortlist"
- Send a confidential search request to landlords, developers and our network in [markets]
- Identify suitable existing buildings and development projects, including options that are not openly marketed
- Test each option against the requirement, starting with [the deciding criteria]
- Compile a longlist of suitable options and advise on the market rent at each location
- Agree a shortlist with [Client] and arrange site visits

H3 "Phase 2: Proposals and negotiation"
- Draft the Request for Proposal (RFP) based on [Client]'s requirements
- Analyse the proposals received and prepare a financial comparison
- Negotiate rent, term, [specific items] and the delivery date with landlords and developers
- Draft counter proposals and update the financial comparison

H3 "Phase 3: Contracting and delivery"
- Draft the Letter of Intent
- Negotiate the commercial terms of the lease agreement and support [Client]'s legal advisers in finalising it
- Attend the handover and check the delivery report

Closing paragraph:
> The overview above is a brief outline of our services and will be specified in more detail
> as the process develops. Where needed, we can coordinate other parties, such as
> [technical consultants], environmental consultants and lawyers. All steps take place in
> close dialogue with [Client], and you will be kept updated through calls, written updates
> and meetings.

Purchase or build-to-suit instead of lease: replace "lease agreement" with "purchase
agreement" or "development agreement" and add site/permitting items to Phase 1 (meetings
with local government, as in the RO 2023 letter).

### Capital markets (sale and leaseback, investment sale)

Use stages instead of phases, e.g. Stage 1 "Shape the lease and the price", Stage 2
"Secure the investor", Stage 3 "Due diligence and close", plus a separate H3 for any
support the client leads itself. Explain the transaction structure in a short
"The transaction" section with a "Sequence" H3 and bullets. See the Polish capital markets letter
pattern: the property table, the transaction, the services, outside our scope.

## 5. Outside our scope

> So that there is no misunderstanding, the following do not form part of this instruction:

Standard bullets (pick what applies, add deal-specific ones):
- Technical due diligence, including structural surveys [and specialist items]
- Legal advice, contract drafting and permitting
- Tax, customs and accounting advice
- Any formal valuation of a property
- [Anything the client or another adviser leads]

## 6. Our team

H1 "Our team", "The contact details for our team are as follows:", then a three-column
table Name / Role / Email, then one sentence on who leads and who does what:
> [Lead] leads the instruction and is [Client]'s single point of contact. The local teams run
> the market search, site visits and landlord negotiations in their own markets.

## 7. Remuneration (fee table: Item / Fee)

Never pick a fee structure or number yourself. Ask. The variants used so far:

**A. Landlord pays, no client top-up (UK-contracted occupier letter, 2026)**
> **Paid by the landlord.** The fee is paid by the landlord or developer of the facility
> [Client] leases, on terms agreed between CBRE and that landlord or developer. No fee is
> payable by [Client].
> CBRE confirms the fee arrangement with the landlord or developer before [Client] signs a
> Letter of Intent.

**B. Percentage of rent, landlord first, client tops up (NL 2022 and RO 2023 occupier letters)**
> [Country]: [15]% of gross annual rent.
> Fees will be collected from the landlord. If CBRE is not, or only partly, able to obtain a
> fee from the landlord, [Client] will pay CBRE the part that cannot be collected.
RO 2023 payment split: one third after signing the lease, two thirds after delivery of the
premises.

**C. Capital markets (PL capital markets letter, 2026)**
> **[1.7]% net** of the transaction value, meaning the price achieved on the sale to the
> investor. The fee is earned and payable on completion.

**Abortive fee options**
- None: "None. No fee is payable by [Client] if it decides not to proceed or if the search does not lead to a lease."
- Single: "Should the process be terminated after entering Phase 2, an abortive fee of €[20,000] applies." (NL 2022)
- Staged: €[5,000] after entering Phase 2, a further €[5,000] in Phase 3, €[10,000] in total. (RO 2023)

**Third-party costs** (keep unless told otherwise; flag it if the client should pay nothing):
> CBRE is not responsible for the costs of third parties instructed in connection with the
> [search], including legal, tax and technical advisers [and specialists].
> [Client] reimburses CBRE for out-of-pocket costs incurred during the engagement that
> [Client] has approved in advance in writing and that we document.

## 8. Standard sections

**VAT**
> All fees and expenses are exclusive of VAT, which will be charged at the applicable rate.

**Payment of fees and expenses** (adapt to fee variant)
> [Who is invoiced and when.] Our invoices are due for payment within 30 days of issue. We
> reserve the right to charge interest calculated on a daily basis from the 31st day
> following the date of issue at the statutory rate of interest, and to charge any reasonable
> debt collection costs we incur in the recovery of amounts properly due.

**The process and exclusivity**
> CBRE acts exclusively for [Client] on this [search] and represents only the interests of
> [Client]. CBRE will not act as adviser to, or provide services to, any landlord, developer
> or other third party in connection with this instruction unless [Client] agrees otherwise
> in writing. [If landlord-paid: The fact that the landlord pays the fee does not change
> this.] In practice this exclusivity means the following:
- CBRE is the exclusive real estate adviser for this [search]; no other real estate consultant will be instructed on it
- CBRE is the point of contact towards landlords, developers and agents on behalf of [Client]
- CBRE coordinates the [search], the negotiations and the finalisation of the commercial terms, in line with the section "The services" above
- If a landlord, developer or third-party consultant approaches [Client] directly in connection with this [search], [Client] will refer them to CBRE

**Confidentiality** (include when the client asks for confidentiality)
> CBRE treats this instruction as strictly confidential. We will not disclose [Client]'s name
> to landlords, developers or agents without [Client]'s prior consent, and all search
> requests to the market will be issued without naming [Client].

**Term**
> This agreement takes effect on the date of signing and runs for 12 (twelve) months. After
> that period it may be continued by a written amendment signed by both parties.
> If, within 12 (twelve) months after the end of the agreement, [Client] leases a facility
> introduced by CBRE, [CBRE remains entitled to its fee]. On termination, [Client] and CBRE
> will draw up a list of the facilities to which this applies.
> Either party may terminate the agreement at any time on 30 (thirty) days written notice.
> [Abortive fee sentence matching the fee table.]

## 9. Closing

> If you have any questions or would like a further explanation of the contents of this
> letter, please do not hesitate to contact us.
> We look forward to working with your organisation.

Then the approval block (signable letters only) and any enclosure lines.
